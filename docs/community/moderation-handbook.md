# Moderation handbook

> Draft for the owner to adapt. The community guidelines page (`/community/guidelines`) is also a
> draft and needs owner and legal review before launch.

## Who moderates

- Administrators can always moderate.
- Other staff can moderate once an administrator ticks **Community moderator** on their staff
  record in `/admin`.
- The console is at `/moderation`. The CMS dashboard has a link to it.
- Only administrators can allow search-engine indexing of trips, activities and hubs, change
  community settings, or open the test inbox.

## Daily routine (about 15–30 minutes at launch)

1. Open `/moderation`. Check *Waiting for a decision* and *Oldest item waiting*. Aim to clear new
   posts within one day: members wait for approval before anything appears.
2. **Review queue**, oldest first. For each post open the review page:
   - Is it first-hand and useful to other travellers? Is the destination right?
   - Risk flags at the top: links, booking or source links, declared commercial interest, new
     accounts, earlier rejections, open reports.
   - For activities: is it real (check the source link), lawful, safe to list, and does the
     organiser's private contact look genuine? Moderation is not a safety check; the page says so.
   - For edits: the comparison shows what changed. The public version stays as it was until you
     approve.
3. Decide:
   - **Approve and publish**.
   - **Request changes**, with a clear note. The member sees it and can fix and resubmit. Use this
     for fixable problems: a missing destination, a promotional tone, a missing disclosure.
   - **Reject**, with a reason. Use it for spam, off-topic posts, copied text, or anything
     against the guidelines.
4. **Replies**: approve or reject. The same standards apply, in short form.
5. **Reports**: open the item, act on it (hide, remove, restrict), then mark the report
   *resolved*, or *dismiss* it if no action is needed.
6. **Destination suggestions**: approve to create the place, merge it into an existing place, or
   reject.

Every decision is recorded in the **Audit log** with your name, the time and the reason.

## Urgent removals

Act straight away, before anything else, for:
- someone's private information (address, phone, ID, photos of identifiable private people);
- threats, harassment or hate;
- illegal content, scams or dangerous advice;
- photos that should not be public.

On the review page use **Hide** (reversible) or **Remove**. Removal takes the page offline (404) and
out of search and the sitemap at once. Photos can be rejected or removed one by one.

For anything involving a child's safety, a credible threat, or a legal demand, escalate to the
owner (Jeeva) the same day. Do not delete the audit trail. Keep a note of what you saw.

## Account restrictions

On `/moderation/members/<id>`:
- **Suspend** with an end date and a reason. A suspended member can still sign in and read, but
  cannot post, reply, vote, RSVP or upload. Their profile is hidden. Lift it early if appropriate.
- **Trusted** marks members whose posts you have reviewed many times. It currently changes nothing
  automatically unless the owner switches on *Replies from trusted members published without
  review*.
- Repeated spam: reject the posts, then suspend for a long period. There is no permanent ban
  button. Use a long end date and record the reason.

## Indexing (administrators)

- Questions become indexable automatically once they have the set number of approved answers
  (default 1).
- Trip reports and activities stay `noindex` until an administrator ticks **Allow indexing** on the
  review page. Allow it only for genuinely useful, original, first-hand posts.
- Destination hubs need *Hub indexable* on the destination record and enough content.
- Traveller profiles need at least two published posts (setting).

## Appeals

There is no in-app appeal yet. Members are told to use the contact page. Re-review with a
different moderator where possible, and record the outcome in the member's audit trail with a
reason.
